Blockage Removal Guide

Types of Blockages:
1. Sediment buildup - Sand, silt, clay
2. Organic matter - Roots, leaves, algae
3. Debris - Plastic, stones, trash

Detection Signs:
- Reduced water flow
- Water backing up
- Unusual noises
- Visible debris

Removal Methods:
1. Manual removal - For large debris
2. Flushing - High-pressure water
3. Chemical treatment - For organic growth
4. Mechanical cleaning - Augers, scrapers

Prevention:
- Install screens and filters
- Regular cleaning schedule
- Proper disposal of waste
- Monitor water quality

Expert Advice:
- For severe blockages, use professional equipment
- Document blockage locations for pattern analysis
- Consider upgrading to self-cleaning systems
