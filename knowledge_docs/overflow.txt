Overflow Management Guide

Causes of Overflow:
1. Excessive water pressure
2. Blocked drainage channels
3. Malfunctioning valves
4. Heavy rainfall

Immediate Actions:
- Reduce water flow
- Open emergency outlets
- Check for blockages

Prevention:
- Install water level sensors
- Regular channel cleaning
- Pressure regulation
- Emergency overflow design

Best Practices:
- Design channels with 20% extra capacity
- Install warning systems
- Regular maintenance schedule
- Train staff on emergency procedures
