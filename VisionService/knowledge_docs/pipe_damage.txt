Pipe Damage Repair Guide

Types of Pipe Damage:
1. Cracks - Minor surface cracks that may spread
2. Breaks - Complete separation of pipe sections
3. Leaks - Water escaping from joints or holes
4. Corrosion - Pipe material degrading over time

Immediate Actions:
- Stop water source immediately
- Mark the leak location
- Assess damage severity

Repair Steps:
1. Excavate around the damaged area
2. Clean the pipe surface
3. Apply repair clamp or replace section
4. Test pressure
5. Backfill and restore

Prevention Tips:
- Regular inspection every 3 months
- Replace old pipes (over 10 years)
- Insulate pipes in winter
- Maintain proper water pressure

Expert Notes:
- For PVC pipes, use appropriate solvent cement
- Metal pipes may need welding or replacement
- Always use pressure-rated fittings
- Document repair for future reference

Case Study: A 30-year-old irrigation system had multiple leaks. Replacing corroded sections reduced water loss by 40%.
